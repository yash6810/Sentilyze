# Quantitative Baselines Experiment Results

## 1. Methodology & Disclosures

- **Universe Composition & Survivorship Bias**: Universe constructed from `stocks.txt` (481 tickers included with complete history from 2018-01-02 to 2026-10-26, 44 excluded due to post-2018 IPO or missing history). **State plainly that the universe is today's constituents, so survivorship bias inflates all stock strategies.**
- **Zero-Sentiment Limitation**: Sentiment features (`mean_sentiment_score`, `positive`, `negative`, `neutral`) were set to 0.0 for the ML strategy because no real news history exists for this historical period.
- **Execution Friction & Transaction Costs**: All trades execute at the NEXT trading day's Open price based on signals generated at the decision day Close. Costs: **0.10% broker fee + 0.05% slippage = 0.15% (0.0015)** applied to every buy and sell traded notional.
- **Survivorship-Free Baselines**: (g) Sector ETF Momentum (top 3 of 10 SPDR sector ETFs, XLC dropped due to mid-2018 inception); (h) Multi-Asset Trend (SPY, EFA, EEM, AGG, GLD 200-day SMA trend filter, 20% max weight each).
- **Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) Audit**: The DSR column was removed from primary performance tables because the required input `var_sharpe` cannot be justified as an identical strategy trial variance across heterogeneous baseline regimes (see Item 7 audit).
